About FA Vision Enterprise

We are a furniture workshop and showroom in Odorkor, Accra. Every piece is built by skilled local craftsmen using seasoned hardwood, quality foam and durable fabrics.

From a single bedside table to fully furnished offices, we focus on solid construction, clean finishing and honest pricing.

  • Quality materials Seasoned local hardwood and high-density foam.
  • Made to measure Custom sizes, colours and fabrics.
  • Delivery & setup Across Accra and surrounding areas.
  • After-sales support Repairs and re-upholstery available.
