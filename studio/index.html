    Questions

    Good to know

    Is it really done by AI?

    We use AI tools to draft wording, create room looks and design faster. Then a person on our team checks and finishes every job before we send it, so you get something correct and ready to use.

    How do I pay?

    Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AT Money) or Visa / Mastercard. You can also pay 50% now and the rest when your work is ready, pay everything when it's ready, or pay at our Odorkor, Omanjor or Kasoa showroom. Guides are paid before we send them.

    Do I get my room makeover fee back?

    Yes. If you buy GH₵1,000 or more of furniture from us within 30 days, the plan fee is taken off your furniture bill. The bulk setup fee is credited in full when you place the order.

    Can you print my programme, brochure or flyer?

    Yes. F.A Vision runs a printing press. Tell us how many copies you need in your brief and we'll quote printing separately.

    What happens to my photos and details?

    We only use them for your job. We don't post your photos or share your details without asking you first.
